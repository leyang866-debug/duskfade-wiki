## Confirmed platforms

Duskfade is available on PC through Steam, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2. Xbox supports Xbox Play Anywhere, and Steam Deck is verified.

## Unsupported systems

There is no Duskfade version for PS4, Xbox One, or the original Nintendo Switch. Confirm the console generation before buying.

## Digital purchase details

PS5 and Xbox listings are digital only. No physical disc, cartridge, limited edition, or collector's edition has been confirmed.

## Choose your version

Pick the library and hardware where a movement-heavy action-platformer feels best to you. The single-player game is the same core adventure on every confirmed platform.
