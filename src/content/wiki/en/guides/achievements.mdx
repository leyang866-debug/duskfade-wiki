## Platform totals

Xbox and PC have 25 achievements worth 1000 Gamerscore. PS5 has 26 trophies: 12 Bronze, 5 Silver, 8 Gold, and 1 Platinum. Use your platform list for exact labels.

## Follow the main categories

The verified groups are story, boss, exploration, collection, and upgrades. Finish the main path before trying to clean every remaining goal, because later movement tools improve return-trip efficiency.

## Track collection goals

All Kittens, Music Boxes, Card Collection, and Rainbow Ingots are major collection goals. Broken Stained Glass connects to Master XII's Secret, so include it in a mastery-focused route.

## Named milestones

Verified names include Cat Army, Master of Melodies, Collector, Advanced Buyer, Master Clockmaker, Master XII's Secret, and Kill Wilson. Do not assume hidden requirements from names alone.
