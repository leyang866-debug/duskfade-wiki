## What bosses test

Each ecological zone has a mid-area boss and a final boss. Encounters mix combat, dodging, and platforming, so a safe route through the arena matters as much as damage. Keep a movement lane open and take short punishes instead of chasing a long combo.

## Confirmed encounters

The confirmed encounter names are Wrath, Guayota, and The Watcher. This is a confirmed list, not a promise that every boss or mid-area encounter has been publicly named. Treat each arena as a movement test as well as a fight.

## Prepare before the arena

Before entering an arena, make sure you can see a retreat lane and have a traversal option available. If the arena asks for a new movement tool, use that tool to create distance first; damage comes after the pattern is readable.

## Guayota: watch the flames

Guayota is safest when you watch the flame pattern before spending Dodge. Move toward the clear direction, then punish only after the attack has passed. Repeating this observation cycle is more reliable than trying to force damage during the pattern.

## Practice attack patterns

The four difficulty settings change how much time you have to read attacks. Master Clockmaker is the highest setting and is best treated as a pattern-learning challenge. On any setting, reset at a safe point after a mistake instead of turning one missed dodge into a full combo.

## Boss guide FAQ

### Which Duskfade bosses are confirmed?

Wrath, Guayota, and The Watcher are the confirmed named encounters covered here.

### Is Guayota mainly a combat or movement check?

Both. Read the flame pattern, keep a movement lane open, and use Dodge only when the safe direction is clear.

### What should I do if a boss arena feels too difficult?

Lower the difficulty long enough to learn the attack pattern, then return to a higher setting once the movement sequence is familiar.
