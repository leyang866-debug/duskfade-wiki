## What bosses test

Each ecological zone has a mid-area boss and a final boss, and encounters mix combat, dodging, and platforming. Confirmed names are Wrath, Guayota, and The Watcher. Keep a movement lane open, take short safe punishes, and use a new traversal tool when the arena asks for it. Against Guayota, observe the flame pattern first and save Dodge until the safe direction is clear. Master Clockmaker gives the deepest pattern-learning challenge.
