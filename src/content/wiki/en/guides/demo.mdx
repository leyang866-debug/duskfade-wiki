## Where to play

The demo is available on PC through Steam and on PS5. It is a practical way to test movement rhythm and camera feel.

## Demo length

Expect roughly one to one-and-a-half hours. It is a condensed first-level experience, not a separate side mode.

## Wrath boss encounter

The demo includes Wrath. Use it to practice clean Dodge timing, safe positioning, and movement through an arena.

## What the full game adds

The full adventure adds the complete world, more abilities, bosses, collectibles, and story.
