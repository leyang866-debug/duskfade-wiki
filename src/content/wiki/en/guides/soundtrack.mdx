## Soundtrack price

The standalone Duskfade soundtrack costs $9.99 on Steam.

## Composer and style

Rafa del Olmo combines orchestral, metal, electronic, and ethnic influences. The score uses leitmotifs for characters and story elements.

## Boss tracks

Known tracks include Guayota Theme and The Watcher. These correspond to confirmed boss encounters without suggesting a complete roster.

## Music in the game

The game includes a music minigame and Tick Town music playback. Music Boxes can be delivered to Arpeggio during exploration.
