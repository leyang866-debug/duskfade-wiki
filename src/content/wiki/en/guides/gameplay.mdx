## Build your movement chain

Duskfade rewards connected movement. Link jumps, dashes, grapples, rails, glides, and attacks instead of resetting after each landing. Use lock-on for a single threat, then release it when the camera or arena needs a wider view. Bosses combine combat and platforming, so preserve a route to safety before committing to a combo. The four difficulty settings let you choose how closely you study attack patterns; Master Clockmaker is the highest setting.
