## Age ratings

Duskfade is rated ESRB E10+ for Fantasy Violence and Mild Blood, and PEGI 7 in Europe.

## Combat content

Combat uses a stylized clockwork sword against fantasy enemies rather than realistic violence. The rating still identifies light blood imagery.

## Family-friendly strengths

The larger adventure mixes Clockpunk exploration, a mechanical bird companion, collectibles, and movement challenges with combat.

## Practical checklist

Consider a player's comfort with light action violence, 3D camera control, and timing-based platforming. The PC and PS5 demo can help test the fit.
