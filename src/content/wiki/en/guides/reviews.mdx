## Scores at a glance

Duskfade has a Metacritic score of 79 from 15 critic reviews and a user score of 8.6 from 27 ratings. The critic split is 67% positive and 33% mixed.

## What critics praise

PSX Brasil gave 95, ElDesmarque 88, TheGamer 80, and MeriStation 8/10. Reviewers praise fluid movement, rewarding exploration, attractive art, and platforming.

## What to keep in mind

MeriStation found combat simpler and repetitive. The Sixth Axis gave 7/10 and noted camera concerns and Switch 2 performance.

## Who should consider Duskfade

It suits players seeking a PS2-era-inspired 3D action-platformer with movement, exploration, melee combat, and hybrid boss arenas.
