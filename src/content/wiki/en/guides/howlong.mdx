## Main-story time

A main-story run takes roughly 10–15 hours. Difficulty, boss learning, and movement practice move the result within that range.

## Casual exploration

Main story plus casual collecting takes around 12–15 hours. Continue the story before trying to clean every locked route.

## Deep exploration

Deep exploration or full collection takes around 15–20+ hours. Group return trips by collectible type and tool requirement.

## Media reference

MeriStation completed 73% in 11 hours and 13 minutes. Treat this as context, not a promise of a personal time.

## Playtime FAQ

### How long is the Duskfade story?

Expect roughly 10–15 hours for a main-story run, depending on difficulty, boss learning, and movement practice.

### How long does it take to collect everything?

Deep exploration or full collection takes around 15–20+ hours. The exact result depends on how many return trips you make.

### Is casual exploration much longer than the story?

Main story plus casual collecting takes around 12–15 hours, so it adds time without requiring a full cleanup route.
