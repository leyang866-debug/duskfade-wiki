## Choose a learning-friendly difficulty

Choose a difficulty that gives you time to read attacks and practice Dodge timing. If a fight is teaching you a pattern, a lower setting can be a useful learning step rather than a permanent choice.

## Keep momentum as your safety net

Link jumps, dashes, grapples, and glides when the next landing is readable. Reset at a safe point when the camera or route becomes unclear; preserving a clean position is more useful than forcing momentum.

## Mark routes for return trips

Mark routes that need a later tool instead of repeatedly testing the same blocked path. Use teleporters for focused return trips once the movement upgrade is available.

## Collect with a purpose

Prioritize Upgrade Gears, health, or energy when they solve your current problem. Cosmetics can wait for a dedicated cleanup route, especially when a boss or traversal upgrade is blocking progress.

## Know the family suitability

Duskfade is rated ESRB E10+ for Fantasy Violence and Mild Blood and PEGI 7. The PC and PS5 demo is a practical way to test comfort with light action violence, 3D camera control, and timing-based platforming.

## Beginner tips FAQ

### What difficulty should a new player choose?

Choose the setting that gives you enough time to read attacks and practice Dodge timing. You can raise it after the movement pattern feels familiar.

### Should I collect everything on the first visit?

No. Mark blocked routes and return with the required movement tool instead of searching every corner before the route is accessible.

### What should I upgrade first?

Prioritize Upgrade Gears, health, or energy when one of them directly solves your current boss or traversal problem.
