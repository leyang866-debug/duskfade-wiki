## Choose a learning-friendly difficulty

Choose a difficulty that gives you time to read attacks and practice Dodge timing. Keep purposeful momentum through jumps, dashes, grapples, and glides, but reset at safe points instead of rushing blindly. Mark routes that need a later tool and use teleporters for focused return trips. Prioritize Upgrade Gears, health, or energy when they solve your current problem. Duskfade is ESRB E10+ for Fantasy Violence and Mild Blood and is PEGI 7.
