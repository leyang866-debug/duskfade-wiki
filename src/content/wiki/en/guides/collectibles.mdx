## Plan collection runs

Duskfade rewards return trips. Mark routes that are out of reach, then use teleporters after a new movement tool makes that route practical. Choose one collection goal for each return instead of searching every corner without a plan.

## All known collectible types

Music Boxes go to Arpeggio. N1-C0ins support Card Collection, and Thieving Kittens unlock skins. Upgrade Gears work with Crystals for upgrades, Clock Heart Pieces increase health, and Star Core Fragments increase energy. Flasks of Dye, Rainbow Ingots, Echoes, and Broken Stained Glass support cosmetics, lore, and Master XII's Secret.

## Prioritize useful upgrades

When a boss or traversal problem blocks progress, prioritize Upgrade Gears, health, and energy. Cosmetics can wait for a dedicated cleanup route. This keeps exploration useful without slowing the main adventure.

## Finish with return trips

Kittens, Music Boxes, Card Collection, and Rainbow Ingots are major completion goals. Return with the tools you need, clear one category at a time, and do not infer exact locations that have not been verified.

No. Mark routes that are out of reach and return after a new movement tool makes them practical.
